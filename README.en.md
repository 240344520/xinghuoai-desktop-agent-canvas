## Why Xinghuo AI

- **Free and local-first** — Download and use Xinghuo AI locally for free. Your canvas, asset management, media preview, and editing workflow stay centered on your Mac instead of being locked into a single web product.
- **A polished, calm interaction model** — Canvas pan and zoom, node selection and dragging, media preview, parameter editing, and creation collaboration are designed to feel responsive, focused, and unobtrusive.
- **Bring your own models** — Connect mainstream cloud-model APIs and OpenAI-compatible endpoints, or run local models such as Ollama. Choose the model and provider that fit each task.
- **One infinite canvas for every creative asset** — Keep images, video, audio, text, prompts, parameters, and results together in a visual workflow you can see, organize, and continue.
- **AI Agent collaboration and extensible workflows** — Agents understand the current canvas context. Connect tools with MCP and reuse creative methods through Skills to build a workflow that fits you.

> Xinghuo AI itself is free to download and use locally. Cloud models, external MCP services, and some extensions use your own provider accounts and credentials; availability, limits, and charges are determined by each provider.

## Built for

- Creators who want one place to organize images, video, audio, text, and creative results.
- Designers, content teams, and indie developers who need a visual way to connect assets, parameters, and AI creation flows.
- macOS users who want local asset management while optionally using image, video, audio, or music generation services.

## Download and install

Download the latest DMG from [Releases](https://github.com/240344520/xinghuoai-desktop-agent-canvas/releases/latest):

- `arm64` — Apple Silicon Macs (M-series chips).
- `x64` — Intel Macs.

Open the DMG and drag “薪火 AI” into Applications. If macOS shows a security prompt on the first launch, follow the system guidance to confirm the app source.

After installation, create a canvas and add local assets. Image, video, audio, and music generation become available when you configure the corresponding service, account, and quota in the app.

## Highlights

- **Canvas projects** — Create and enter local canvas projects, using nodes to organize assets and results.
- **Multimedia nodes** — Work with images, text, video, and audio in the same canvas.
- **Infinite canvas** — Freely pan and zoom while arranging assets, parameters, tasks, and outputs.
- **AI multimedia creation** — When a service is configured, start image, video, audio, or music generation tasks from the canvas.
- **Node tools** — Upload, edit, enhance, preview fullscreen, create templates, and download from familiar node actions.
- **Asset management** — Browse local images, video, and audio, then bring them back into your canvas workflow.
- **Agent collaboration** — Describe a creative goal in canvas context, review results, and keep moving the work forward.
